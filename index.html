04 — Research & Projects

Research & Projects

2026 ICME

TAFI: Temporal Augmentation with Frame Interpolation for Multi-Object Tracking

Advisors: Chien-Yao Wang, Po-Chyi Su

A novel, training-free architecture that overcomes low-frame-rate limitations in multi-object tracking (MOT). TAFI uses Video Frame Interpolation (VFI) to generate intermediate frames, repairing the trajectory associations that low frame rates tend to break — without retraining the tracker or the detector.

+14.3 HOTA on ByteTrack
(sampling interval 30)
+8.8 HOTA on ByteTrack
(sampling interval 10)
0 Extra training required
(training-free design)

Evaluated on top of both ByteTrack and CenterTrack across multiple VFI backbones (FILM, RIFE, SVD), consistently improving HOTA, IDF1 and MOTA while reducing identity switches versus the baselines.

Multi-Object Tracking Video Frame Interpolation Video Diffusion Training-free Computer Vision

PPI Interactor Design

Generative Artificial Intelligence and Foundation Models in Bioinformatics: Theory and Applications

A three-stage generative pipeline for de novo protein-protein interaction (PPI) design: a GraphSAGE-based GNN trained on ESM-2 embeddings to predict interactions, an autoregressive Transformer (mini-GPT) to generate novel protein sequences, and a ranking + ESMFold folding-validation stage that filters candidates into exportable FASTA/PDB structures.
